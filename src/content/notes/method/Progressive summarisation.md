---
title: Progressive summarisation
tags: [method/reading]
created: 2026-03-14
updated: 2026-05-30
---

Highlight a passage. Later, bold the best of the highlights. Later still,
summarise the bold in your own words.

Each pass happens only when you return to the note for some other reason, so
the notes you actually use get compressed and the ones you do not are left
alone. The effort follows the attention instead of leading it.

Compare [[Atomic notes]], which compresses at write time rather than at read
time.
