## The rule

One note, one idea. If you cannot title a note without using "and", it is two
notes.

## Why

A note holding one idea can be linked from anywhere that idea is relevant. A
note holding six can only be linked as a lump, and the five ideas you did not
want come along with the one you did.

Atomicity is what makes [[Zettelkasten]] linking work at all. It is also why
[[Obsidian]]'s wikilink syntax matters more than it looks: if linking is
frictionless, you write more, smaller notes.
