German sociologist, 1927–1998. Built the [[Zettelkasten]] that produced roughly
seventy books and four hundred articles.

Asked how he was so productive, he said he was not: the slipbox was. He
described working with it as a conversation: he would ask it something and it
would answer with a card he had forgotten writing.
